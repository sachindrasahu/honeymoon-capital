About the company

Sachindra & Stuti Honeymoon Ltd. was incorporated after a long period of due diligence (dating), followed by a highly publicised merger (the wedding) on 5 and 7 February 2027. The merger was approved by both families, several aunties, and one neighbour who was not consulted but approved anyway.

The company is now raising a fresh issue to fund its first and most important international expansion: a honeymoon in Italy. The company has no revenue, no plans for revenue, and a very strong pipeline of pasta.
